❓ How to play

Drop bombs to blast the crates and trap the other bombers. A bomb goes off after a couple of seconds, shooting flames in a + shape. Flames break crates, set off other bombs, and knock out anyone they touch – including you! Last bomber standing wins the round.
Move: joystick / arrow keys / WASD / D-pad   Bomb: BOMB button / Space / J / controller A
Power-ups hide inside crates:
Extra bomb – drop one more at a time
Bigger blast – your flames reach further
Speed boots – run faster
Bomb kick – walk into a bomb to send it sliding
Shield – survives one blast
After 2 minutes the arena starts closing in, so keep moving! In Party mode, phones join by scanning the QR code on the TV.
