❓ How to play

Knock the other fighters off the arena! Every hit adds to their damage %, and the higher it gets, the further they fly. Fall off the screen and you lose a life. Last fighter standing wins.
MoveJoystick ◀ ▶ (keyboard: A D or arrows). Hold ▼ on a thin platform to drop through.
JumpJUMP. Press again in the air for a double jump (Bonk gets three!)
PunchPUNCH (keyboard: J). Running punch = dash. In the air hold ▼ + PUNCH = ground-slam
SpecialSPECIAL (keyboard: K) – every fighter has their own, and it helps you get back to the arena
🤖 PipAll-rounder. Spring Rocket: launches up, hitting anyone in the way.
🪨 BoulderHeavy power. Quake Slam: a shockwave rolls along the platform.
🦛 BonkHeavy bruiser. Belly Bounce: a hop and a huge belly-flop. Three jumps.
🦎 ZippaSpeedster. Tail Whip spin. Clings to the side of the arena – press JUMP to wall-jump (3 times).
🟣 GloopTrickster. Stretchy long punch. Puddle Dodge: melts, slides, then springs up with an uppercut.
🐧 FrostbiteControl. Ice Slide leaves slippery ice. In the air it throws a snowball.
🏆 Arcade: pick a fighter and beat the other five one after another, then the five bosses from Pip – King Gloop, Captain Clank, Yeti-Bot, Scrap King and Queen Buzz. Your lives carry on between fights (beating a boss gives you an extra one).
🤝 Boss Raid: team up with family (phones) or computer helpers and take on the bosses together. Bosses have a health bar – hit them as much as you can, and hit them extra hard when they're dizzy!
Keyboard: move A D / ← →, jump W / ↑ / Space, punch J, special K, pause P. Game controllers work too (A jump, X punch, B special).
